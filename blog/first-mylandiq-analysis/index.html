Your First MyLandIQ Analysis: From Address to Investment Intelligence in 90 Seconds

"The difference between a profitable investment and a catastrophic one is not luck — it is the 90 seconds you spend analysing before you commit."

You have heard about MyLandIQ. You have downloaded the app. You have 5 free credits sitting in your account. Now what?

This guide walks you through your very first property analysis — step by step — and more importantly, teaches you how to read and act on the results. By the end, you will understand exactly what the AI is telling you about any piece of land in India.

The 4 Steps

1

Enter the Property Address

Type the full address of the property you are evaluating. The more specific, the better.

Good: "Plot 47, GT Road, near Civil Hospital, Hoshiarpur, Punjab"
Better: Use the 📍 "Use My Current Location" button if you are standing on the plot — the app captures GPS coordinates and reverse-geocodes the exact address.
💡 Pro tip: Include landmarks (near bus stand, opposite XYZ hospital, GT Road facing) — this helps the AI determine road frontage and neighbourhood type more accurately.

The AI automatically detects the city, state, country, and city tier (Tier 1 Metro to Tier 5 Rural) from the address. You do not need to specify these manually.

2

Enter the Plot Size

Enter the total area of the plot and select your preferred unit:

Supported units: Square Yards (most common in Punjab), Square Feet, Square Metres, Acres, Hectares, Kanal, Marla, Bigha, Gaj
💡 Pro tip: Plot size directly affects which projects are recommended. A 200 sq yard plot will show individual houses and clinics. A 2,000 sq yard plot will additionally show warehouses, apartment buildings, and campus-scale projects. Be accurate — overestimating your plot size will generate recommendations you cannot actually build.
3

Tap "Analyse" and Wait 30 Seconds

This is where the magic happens. In the background, the engine is doing the following simultaneously:

  • Detecting city tier — classifying the location as Tier 1 (Metro) through Tier 5 (Rural) and adjusting all cost and revenue benchmarks accordingly
  • Reading area character — determining whether the neighbourhood is residential, commercial, industrial, mixed-use, or agricultural based on surrounding landmarks
  • Pulling city intelligence — loading local market data for that specific city: what projects thrive, what fails, what is trending
  • Scoring 52 project types — evaluating every possible use (from individual house to data centre) across the 4-Pillar framework: Legal, Physical, Demand, and Financial
  • Running financial models — calculating construction costs, revenue projections, ROI, and payback period for each viable project, calibrated to local rates
  • Filtering and ranking — eliminating impossible options (plot too small, wrong zone, negative ROI) and ranking the rest by composite score

All of this happens in under 30 seconds. The equivalent human research — visiting the site, consulting a builder, meeting a CA, calling a lawyer — would take weeks and cost lakhs.

4

Read Your Results

The results screen shows you everything you need to make an informed decision. Here is how to read each section:

Understanding Your Results

The 4-Pillar Composite Score

At the top of your results, you will see the overall score for each recommended project. Here is what the ranges mean:

Sample Analysis: 500 sq yd, GT Road, Hoshiarpur

Top Recommendation: Skill Development & Tech Training Hub 🔥

⚖️ Legal 82/100
🏗️ Physical 78/100
📈 Demand 85/100
💰 Financial 71/100
Composite Score 79/100

Score interpretation:

  • 80-100: Strong candidate. Proceed to professional verification with confidence.
  • 60-79: Viable but with caveats. Check the pillar that scored lowest — that is your risk area.
  • 40-59: Marginal. Consider alternative project types or negotiate a lower land price.
  • Below 40: Not recommended. The analysis explains why — trust it.
  • 🔴 Red Alert: Negative ROI detected. The engine is protecting you from a loss-making investment.

Top Recommendations vs Alternatives

MyLandIQ shows you two groups of results:

  • Top 5 Recommendations — the highest-scoring project types for your specific plot, spanning at least 3 different categories (to give you diverse options, not 5 variations of the same idea)
  • 2-3 Alternatives — lower-scoring but still viable options that might suit your specific goals or budget better

Key insight: The top recommendation is not always the "right" one for you. A warehouse might score highest financially, but if you want passive income with zero management, a plotted development (ranked #3) might be your better personal choice. The score tells you what is optimal — your priorities determine what is right.

The 🔥 Trending Badge

Some recommendations carry a 🔥 badge. This means the project type is nationally trending in 2026 — backed by government policy, venture capital, or explosive market growth. Trending sectors include EV charging, dark stores (quick commerce), data centres, digital healthcare, skill development, and more. These badges are not marketing hype — they are data signals from the City Intelligence Engine that indicate strong and growing demand.

Financial Projections

For each recommended project, you will see:

  • Estimated construction cost — calibrated to your city tier (Tier 1 costs 40-50% more than Tier 3 for the same specification)
  • Total project investment — land cost estimate + construction + regulatory costs
  • Revenue projection — based on local sale rates or rental yields
  • ROI percentage — your expected return on total investment
  • Payback period — how many years to recover the investment

Wait — are these projections guaranteed?

No. These are AI-generated approximations based on publicly available data and algorithmic models. They are a starting point for your own research, not a guarantee. Always verify with a CA, lawyer, and licensed valuer before committing capital. The projections help you compare options and identify red flags — they do not replace professional due diligence.

The Branded PDF Report

Tap "Download PDF" to get a professional report you can share with your CA, business partner, or family. The report includes everything from the results screen, formatted as a structured document with your property details, the 4-Pillar breakdown, financial projections, city market intelligence, risk assessment, and disclaimers.

This is not a screenshot. It is a branded, structured document designed to be the starting point for a professional conversation about the investment. When you sit with your CA and say "I ran an analysis — here are the numbers," you are speaking from data, not speculation.

What to Do After Your First Analysis

  1. Do not buy immediately. The analysis is the beginning of due diligence, not the end. It tells you what to investigate, not what to conclude.
  2. Check the lowest-scoring pillar. If Legal scored low, that is your first investigation — get a title search done. If Demand scored low, talk to local brokers and validate the market.
  3. Run 2-3 analyses on different properties. You have 5 free credits. Use them to compare different plots you are considering. The comparative view is often more valuable than any single analysis.
  4. Share the PDF with your advisor. Whether it is your CA, lawyer, or a trusted family member who knows the local market — the report gives them structured data to work with.
  5. Check alternatives. If the top recommendation is not what you had in mind, scroll down. The alternatives section might show a project type that better matches your personal goals, budget, or risk appetite.

Common First-Timer Questions

Does the analysis use 1 credit every time?

Yes. Each analysis costs 1 credit. You start with 5 free credits on signup. Additional credits can be purchased in packs — and there is a launch offer with bonus credits on every pack.

Can I re-analyse the same property?

Yes, but it will use another credit. This is useful if you want to test a different plot size on the same address (e.g., "what if I buy only 300 sq yards instead of 500?").

Are my analyses saved?

Yes. Every analysis is automatically saved to your account via cloud sync. You can access your full history from the History tab — even on a different device signed into the same account.

What if I get a Red Alert?

A Red Alert means the Financial pillar detected negative ROI — the investment would lose money based on current projections. This is the app protecting you. Read our detailed guide on why negative ROI is not a bug.
