❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Understanding what MyLandIQ does — and what it doesn't.

About MyLandIQ

What is MyLandIQ?

MyLandIQ is an AI-powered analytical tool that provides informational property analysis for land development research. It is not an investment advisor, real estate broker, or professional consultancy service.

Is MyLandIQ registered with SEBI?

No. MyLandIQ and its developer (Rajiv Ohri) are not registered with SEBI as an Investment Adviser or Research Analyst. We do not provide personalized investment advice. The app provides AI-generated informational analysis only.

Is MyLandIQ a RERA-registered real estate agent?

No. We are not registered with any Real Estate Regulatory Authority (RERA). We do not broker properties, facilitate transactions, or provide real estate consultancy services.

Can I rely solely on MyLandIQ for investment decisions?

No. MyLandIQ provides informational analysis only. You should conduct independent due diligence and consult qualified professionals — including SEBI-registered investment advisors, RERA-registered real estate professionals, architects, engineers, and legal counsel — and verify all information independently before making decisions.

Understanding the Analysis

How accurate is the AI analysis?

AI analysis has inherent limitations. It is based on publicly available data that may be outdated or incomplete, can contain errors, provides approximations rather than exact figures, and cannot predict future market conditions. It should never be the sole basis for a decision. Always verify information with professional consultants.

What does the "4-Pillar Analytical Framework" mean?

It is an analytical framework — not an investment rating or recommendation. It provides an informational assessment across Legal, Physical, Demand, and Financial factors to help you organize your research. It does not guarantee investment success or constitute professional investment advice.

Are the ROI scenarios guaranteed?

No. All financial figures are illustrative estimates based on assumptions that may not materialize, are subject to significant variance from actual results, and are not guarantees or promises of returns. Property investment involves risk of loss; actual results may differ dramatically.

Can I use MyLandIQ analysis for loan applications or official purposes?

No. Our AI-generated reports are for informational purposes only and are not professional appraisals, certified valuations, suitable for bank/financial submissions, legal documents, or regulatory compliance documents. For official purposes, obtain certified valuations from licensed professionals.

Responsible Use

Do I still need professionals after using MyLandIQ?

Yes. MyLandIQ is a research tool, not a replacement for professional services. Before investment decisions, consult SEBI-registered investment advisors, chartered accountants, and financial planners. For real estate matters, consult RERA-registered agents, licensed valuers, and real estate attorneys. For development projects, consult licensed architects, engineers, and legal counsel for permits and approvals.

What happens if I lose money on a decision informed by MyLandIQ?

MyLandIQ provides informational analysis with no guarantee of accuracy and accepts no liability for investment outcomes. By using the app, you accept full responsibility for your decisions and the associated risks. See Section 9 (Limitation of Liability) of our Terms of Service.

Is my data shared with advertising companies?

No. We do not sell your personal data. Property data you analyze is sent to Anthropic (Claude AI) for analysis generation and stored in Google Firebase for your account access. It is not shared with advertising companies or used for marketing without your consent. See our Privacy Policy for details.
