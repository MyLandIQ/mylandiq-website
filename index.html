How It Works

Four steps from address to actionable insights

1

Enter Property Details or Tap GPS

Type your property address and land area — or tap "Use My Current Location" for auto-detection. Select your unit (sq ft, sq yd, marla, kanal, bigha). Currency is detected automatically from the address.

2

Tier-Aware Engine Analyzes

MyLandIQ identifies the city from a database of 200+ Indian cities across 5 tiers, detects area character (Commercial, Residential, Industrial), and runs the 4-Pillar scoring algorithm — all on-device in milliseconds.

3

Get Your 4-Pillar Investment Score

See your pillar breakdown (Legal, Physical, Demand, Financial) with individual scores and a weighted overall score out of 100. Financial Alerts warn you about negative ROI before you invest a single rupee.

4

Download PDF & Take Action

Get top 5 ranked project recommendations with cost estimates (in Lakh/Crore), revenue projections, and timelines. Export a branded PDF report to share with partners, banks, or keep for your records.
